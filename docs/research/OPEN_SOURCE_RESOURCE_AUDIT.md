**Muscle Intelligence — Scientific Data & Biomechanics Foundation**

## 2. Synthesis of Resource Categorisation

### 2.1 Primary Ingestion & Integrated Systems (MVP Core)
1. **Kinetic Exercises Database:** Chosen as the primary open-source baseline for exercise names, equipment definitions, and secondary muscle classifications due to its clean MIT licence, modern JSON structure, and zero copyleft contamination risk.
2. **OpenAlex & Crossref:** Chosen as the primary bibliometric backbone for paper metadata, DOI verification, citation networks, and open-access URL discovery under CC0 / open terms.
3. **PubMed / Europe PMC:** Chosen as the primary biomedical query engine to fetch peer-reviewed abstracts and open-access full-text protocol details.
4. **EPIC Lab Open Data:** Chosen as the empirical benchmark dataset for lower-limb bodyweight biomechanics (squats, lunges, stairs) under CC-BY 4.0.

### 2.2 Research Reference Datasets (Offline Calibration)
1. **OpenSim & OpenCap:** Retained strictly as an **offline research environment**. Simulations generate joint moment arms and torque curves that are baked into the `@muscle-intelligence/calculations` engine as deterministic model parameters.
2. **AddBiomechanics:** Referenced offline for ground reaction forces and kinematics during dynamic calisthenics.
3. **Z-Anatomy & BodyParts3D:** Used as the structural reference for the anatomical taxonomy (FMA codes, muscle heads, actions) while keeping 3D polygon meshes out of the MVP client bundle to protect mobile performance and prevent CC-BY-SA license spillover.
4. **JMNS & MyoNeuroLink:** Used as empirical benchmarks for muscle activation ranges without raw ingestion into client state.

### 2.3 Secondary Enrichment & Rejected Resources
1. **wger (Enrichment Only):** Excluded from the application runtime and canonical database core due to AGPL-3.0 copyleft terms. Permitted only as an offline reference for exercise aliases under CC-BY-SA 3.0 with isolated dataset attribution.
2. **OpenExerciseDB (Enrichment Only):** Used solely for supplementary Markdown instructions; not used as the primary schema.
3. **Three.js (Deferred):** Deferred to Phase 8. The client interface will launch with a lightweight, high-performance 2D SVG muscle explorer, avoiding heavy WebGL overhead, shader compilation stalls on low-end mobile devices, and unnecessary complexity.
